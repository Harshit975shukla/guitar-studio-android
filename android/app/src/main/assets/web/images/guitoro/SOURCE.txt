Guitar artwork from guitoro.com (repo Harshit975shukla/guitar-studio), the owner's own site; reuse permitted by the owner Harshit Shukla on 2026-10-24.

Files here are crops (neck + headstock) of guitoro's guitar/acoustic-rebuilt.webp, guitar/nylon-rebuilt.webp and
guitar/electric.webp, made in the browser (canvas, webp quality 0.84). The electric crop has guitoro's headstock flip
(about the neck axis, with its small shear) baked in, as guitoro draws it. The string/fret positions in
src/ui/guitoroNeck.ts come from guitoro's guitar/acoustic.json, nylon.json and electric.json.

lab/ (Guitar Lab 2D view, added 2026-10-24): acoustic-, nylon- and electric-{320,1600}.webp are guitoro's full guitar pictures
(guitar/acoustic-rebuilt.webp, guitar/nylon-rebuilt.webp, guitar/electric.webp), cropped to guitoro's contentBox and resized
(webp quality 80, 320 and 1600 px wide). The electric one has guitoro's headstock flip baked in, as guitoro draws it (same
transform as electric-neck.webp). The clickable part shapes in src/ui/guitarLabGuitoro.ts use guitoro's acoustic/nylon/
electric.json (nut, bridge, tuner points, board edges) and the pictures' own outline (src/ui/guitarLabGuitoroData.ts, traced from the alpha).

<kind>-guitar-320.webp / -1600.webp (acoustic, nylon, electric; 2026-10-24): the whole guitar for the Guitar view,
cropped to the guitar's outline + a small margin and resized with Pillow (LANCZOS, webp quality 80). Same sources;
the electric one uses the same baked headstock flip as electric-neck.webp.

tuner/headstock-acoustic.webp (2026-10-24, Tuner page): the headstock of guitoro's guitar/acoustic-rebuilt.webp, cropped (680 x 425 px, from x 410..1090, y 515..940 of the 4344 x 1448 source), with the start of the neck faded out on the right (Pillow, webp quality 84). Peg positions in src/ui/tunerHeadstock.ts follow the picture's six tuning keys.
